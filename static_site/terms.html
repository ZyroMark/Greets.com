3. Greeters and Greeties

A Greeter books and pays for a meet. A Greetie is booked and paid for their time. The same person may act in both roles from one account.

Greeties set their own rate, choose whether they accept video calls, voice calls or both, and decide which bookings to accept. Declining a booking carries no penalty and needs no explanation.

Greeties are independent of Greets. They are not our employees, agents or partners, and nothing they say during a meet is advice given by us. Professional guidance given in a meet is theirs, not ours, and you rely on it at your own discretion.

4. Bookings and meets

  • Meets are available in four lengths: 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour and 2 hours.
  • A booking is a request until the Greetie accepts it. Once accepted it becomes a confirmed meet.
  • Every meet happens inside Greets on video or voice. Phone numbers, addresses and outside apps are never exchanged.
  • The call type is whatever the Greetie offers. Where they offer both, the Greeter selects at booking.
  • Either person may end a meet early. Ending early because the other person broke the conduct rules is covered in section 6.
  • Greets does not guarantee the quality, usefulness or outcome of any meet.

5. Payments, fees and payouts

  • The price shown at booking is the full price for that meet. It follows the length you chose.

  • Greets deducts a platform fee from the Greetie's earnings. The current fee is shown in your account before you take bookings.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Greetie declines or does not show up. Full refund to the Greeter. Nothing is deducted.

  • Greeter cancels within 24 hours. Partial refund, since the Greetie held the time.
  • A meet is ended because the Greeter broke the conduct rules. No refund, and the Greetie keeps the payment.
  • A meet is ended because the Greetie broke the conduct rules. Full refund to the Greeter, and the payout is withheld.
  • Technical failure on our side. The meet is rescheduled, or refunded in full if that is not possible.
