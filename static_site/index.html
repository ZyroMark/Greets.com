Ground rules

What Greets is, and what it is not

Greets is for conversation, advice, coaching and fan meets. It is not a dating site and there is nothing sexual on it.

🔒

Nothing sexual, ever

Sexual requests, sexual content and nudity are banned for everyone on both sides. One report is enough for us to look, and confirmed cases end the account.

📱

No phone numbers

Calls run inside Greets. No numbers, no addresses, no outside apps. Anyone pushing you to move the conversation elsewhere is breaking the rules.

⚑

Report in one tap

There is a report control on every profile and in the footer of every page. Reports are confidential and the person you report is never told who filed it.

👤

Fans stay anonymous

Greeters sign up with a username. Your legal name is never shown on your profile, in search or to the Greetie you book.

✓

Greeties are verified

Anyone taking money uses their real name and passes identity checks first. You know exactly who you booked before the call starts.

💳

Money is held safely

Payment is taken at booking and released to the Greetie after the meet. No show, no charge, refunded in full.

Anyone can get paid to be met

You do not need to be famous. If people want your time, whether that is a portfolio review, a language chat or an hour of honest advice, you can set a rate and take bookings today.
