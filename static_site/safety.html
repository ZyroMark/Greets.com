Greets only works if people feel safe using it. These are the rules, what happens when someone breaks them, and how to report a problem.

Terms and conditions

What is not allowed

These apply to Greeters and Greeties equally. Paying for a meet does not buy an exception to any of them.

  • Sexual conduct of any kind. Sexual requests, sexual talk, nudity, exposure, sexual gestures, or steering a meet in that direction.
  • Sexual abuse or exploitation. Any attempt to coerce, pressure or manipulate someone sexually. We treat this as the most serious thing that can happen on Greets.
  • Harassment and abuse. Insults, slurs, intimidation, repeated unwanted contact, or targeting someone over who they are.
  • Threats. Threats of violence or harm against anyone, on or off the platform.
  • Asking for private contact details. Phone numbers, home or work addresses, personal email, or messaging handles.
  • Moving the meet off Greets. Asking someone to continue on another app, or to pay outside the platform.
  • Recording without consent. Recording, screenshotting or streaming a meet unless both people have clearly agreed.
  • Impersonation. Claiming to be someone you are not, or booking on someone else's behalf without telling them.
  • Payment abuse. Chargeback fraud, fake no show claims, or pressuring someone to pay more once a meet has started.

How to report someone

There are two ways, and both take a few seconds:

  • Open the person's profile and use the report control at the bottom of the page.
  • Use the Report someone link in the footer of any page on Greets.

Tell us who it was and what happened. You can add detail if you want to, but a report with only a name and a category is still acted on. Reports are confidential. The person you report is never told who filed it, and they do not see the contents.

What happens after a report

  • We look at every report. None of them are closed automatically.
  • Serious reports suspend the account immediately while we review, rather than after.
  • Confirmed sexual conduct, abuse or threats end the account permanently. Pending payouts are withheld and any related bookings are refunded.
  • Criminal conduct is passed to the authorities along with the records we hold about it.
  • You can block someone straight away, whether or not you file a report. Blocking cancels any booking between you and refunds it in full.

If you are a Greeter

  • Keep your username separate from your real name. Do not put your legal name in it.
  • Never send money outside Greets. A Greetie asking you to pay another way is a scam, and you have no protection if you do.
  • You can end a call at any moment for any reason. If someone broke the rules, report it and we will look at a refund.
  • Nobody on Greets needs your phone number, your address or your bank details. Anyone asking is not who they say they are.

If you are a Greetie

  • You choose your call type. If video does not suit you, take voice only and the camera never turns on.
  • You can decline any booking without giving a reason, and it costs you nothing.
  • End a meet the moment someone crosses a line, then report it. You keep the payment for a meet ended because the other person broke the rules.
  • Your address, phone number and bank details are never shown to a Greeter at any point.

If someone is in danger

Greets is not an emergency service and we cannot respond in real time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. Report it to us afterwards and we will preserve the account records and cooperate with any investigation.

One report is enough for us to look. You never need to be certain, and you will not get in trouble for reporting something that turns out to be a misunderstanding.
