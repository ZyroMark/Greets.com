Privacy policy

Last updated 23 August 2026. This explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

Greeters are anonymous to other users behind a username. Greeties show a real name because they are being paid. Nobody's phone number, address or bank details are ever shown to another user. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. What we collect

Everyone

  • Email address and password.
  • Your username, and anything you choose to put on your profile.
  • Bookings you make or receive, including length, call type and price.
  • Reports you file or that are filed about you.
  • Technical data such as device type, browser, approximate region and log records.

Greeties only

  • Real legal name, shown publicly on your profile.
  • Identity verification documents, used to confirm you are who you say you are.
  • Payout details, held by our payment provider so we can pay you.
  • Tax information where the law requires us to collect it.

What we never ask for

  • Your phone number for contacting other users. Greets does not use phone numbers for meets.
  • Your home address, unless a payment or tax rule specifically requires it.
  • Card details in our own systems. Payments are handled by a payment provider.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run your account and let you sign in.
  • To make bookings work and open calls at the right time.
  • To take payments and pay Greeties, and to handle refunds.
  • To verify Greeties, so people know who they are booking and impersonation is caught.
  • To investigate reports and enforce the conduct rules.
  • To meet legal obligations, including tax and cooperating with lawful requests.

3. What other users can see

If you are a Greeter

Other users see your username and anything you deliberately put on your profile. They do not see your real name, your email, your location or your payment details. Your legal name is not shown anywhere on Greets, including to a Greetie you book.

If you are a Greetie

Other users see your real name, your category, your country, your rate, your rating, your availability and your bio. They do not see your email, your address, your payout details or your verification documents.

4. Calls and messages

Meets happen inside Greets on video or voice. We do not record calls as a matter of course, and neither user may record one without the other clearly agreeing.

We keep records of when a meet happened and how long it lasted, because that is what payments and refunds are based on. Messages sent inside Greets are stored so they can be shown to you and, if a report is filed, reviewed by the safety team.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only in these cases:

  • Payment providers, to take payments and send payouts.
  • Identity verification providers, to check Greetie documents.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the platform and run the calls.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it or where we report conduct that appears criminal.

Anyone we share data with is only permitted to use it for the job we gave them.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account is open.
  • When you close your account we delete or anonymise your data, apart from what we must keep.
  • Payment and tax records are kept as long as financial law requires.
  • Safety records tied to a serious report are kept longer, so a removed user cannot simply return.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your data, within the limits in section 6.
  • Restrict or object to certain uses of it.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

Write to privacy@greets.com and we will respond within the period the law sets, and within 30 days at the latest.

8. Security

Data is encrypted moving between you and us and while stored. Passwords are hashed and cannot be read by us. Access to verification documents and payout details is restricted to the small number of staff whose job needs it. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator.

9. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to keep the platform secure. We use a small amount of analytics data to understand which parts of Greets people use. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not let third parties track you across other sites.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or to make a request about your data, contact privacy@greets.com. For safety matters, use the report control on any page or contact safety@greets.com.
