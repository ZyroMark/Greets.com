About Greets

Access to people used to be
impossible to buy

If you follow someone whose work matters to you, there was never a straightforward way to actually speak with them. You could send a message into a pile of thousands and hope. Greets turns that into something simple. They set a price for their time, you book the length you want, and the two of you talk.

Greets is not a dating service

There is nothing sexual on Greets and there never will be. It exists for conversation, advice, coaching, fan meets and professional time. Sexual requests, sexual content, nudity and any form of sexual pressure are banned for everyone on both sides of a booking, whether money changed hands or not.

We act on reports of harassment, threats, sexual abuse and any attempt to pull someone off the platform and into private contact. Confirmed cases end the account permanently, payouts included, and serious cases are passed to the authorities.
