About Greets

Access to people used to be impossible to buy.

If you follow someone whose work matters to you, there was never a straightforward way to actually speak with them. You could send a message into a pile of thousands and hope. Greets turns that into something simple. They set a price for their time, you book the length you want, and the two of you talk.

❤

If you want to meet someone

You are a Greeter. Browse who is taking bookings, pick a length that fits your budget, and pay once. You might want ten minutes with a musician you have followed for years, an hour of portfolio notes from a designer, or a straight answer from a founder who has already done the thing you are about to try.

You sign up under a username. Your real name is never shown on your profile, in search, or to the person you book.

★

If you want to get paid

You are a Greetie. You do not need to be famous to sign up. If people want your time, you can charge for it. Set your own rate, choose whether you take video calls, voice calls or both, and accept only the bookings you want.

Because money changes hands, Greeties sign up under their real legal name and pass an identity check. Greeters know exactly who they are booking.

The meet

You choose the length, and the length sets the price

There are four options. A longer meet costs less per minute, so the person who wants a real conversation is not punished for it.

10 minutes

A quick hello, one question, a signed off moment. The cheapest way to actually speak to someone.

30 minutes

Room for a real conversation. Enough to cover a topic properly without watching a clock.

1 hour

Go deep on something. A full portfolio review, a coaching session, a proper sit down.

2 hours

The longest meet on Greets. For work that needs the time, or a fan meet worth remembering.

✓ Payment is taken when the Greetie accepts and held until the meet happens. If they do not show up, you are refunded in full and nothing is deducted.
Names

One side is anonymous, the other side is not

This is deliberate, and it is the part people ask about most.

Greeters use a username

Fans book under a name they pick. Nobody sees your legal name, and we ask you not to use it. You are paying to speak with someone, not to hand over your identity to do it.

Greeties use their real name

Anyone receiving money uses their real legal name and gets verified before their first payout. That is what stops people impersonating public figures and taking payments for meets that were never going to happen.

🎥

Video or voice

Each Greetie decides what they are comfortable with. Some take video, some take voice only, some take either and let you pick at booking.

📱

No phone numbers

Calls run inside Greets. No numbers, no addresses, no moving to another app. Neither side ever sees a contact detail for the other.

⚑

Reporting on every profile

If something goes wrong, report it in one tap. Reports are confidential and we never tell the other person who filed one.

Say it plainly

Greets is not a dating service

There is nothing sexual on Greets and there never will be. It exists for conversation, advice, coaching, fan meets and professional time. Sexual requests, sexual content, nudity and any form of sexual pressure are banned for everyone on both sides of a booking, whether money changed hands or not.

We act on reports of harassment, threats, sexual abuse and any attempt to pull someone off the platform and into private contact. Confirmed cases end the account permanently, payouts included, and serious cases are passed to the authorities.
